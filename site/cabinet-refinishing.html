Why cabinets need a different process

Kitchen cabinets get handled every day and collect grease you can't see. Paint over that without the right prep and it chips within months.

We degrease, scuff-sand, and use a bonding primer before the finish coats. Doors and drawer fronts come off so every edge gets coated evenly.

Common questions

How long until I can use my kitchen?

You can usually use the kitchen during the job with some limits. Your estimate will give you the timeline for your cabinets.

Can you change wood cabinets to white?

Yes. That's one of the most common requests we get. Proper priming keeps wood grain and tannins from bleeding through.

Do you replace hardware?

We remove and reinstall your existing hardware, or install new hardware you've picked out.
