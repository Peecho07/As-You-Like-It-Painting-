How a job runs

Most of a good paint job happens before the paint comes out.

Prep is where cheap jobs cut corners. It's also where we spend the most time.

  1. Free estimate visit

    We walk the property with you, talk colors and sheen, and note any repairs.

  2. Written estimate

    You get a clear written scope and price. What's included is spelled out.

  3. Protect and prep

    Masking, drop cloths, caulking, sanding, patching, and priming where it's needed.

  4. Finish and walkthrough

    Finish coats, cleanup, and a final walkthrough together before we call it done.

Questions

Homeowner FAQ

Do I need to move my furniture?

We move and cover furniture as part of the job. We just ask that you put away small breakables, wall art, and anything valuable before we arrive.

Can you help me pick colors?

Yes. We bring our experience with what works in Florida light and can bring samples. Many customers test two or three colors on the wall first.

Do you handle HOA paint approvals?

We've worked inside plenty of North County HOAs and can help you put together what your board needs, including approved color schemes.

Can I live in the house while you paint?

Yes. Most of our interior jobs are in occupied homes. We work room by room and keep a clean path through the house.

Ready for a fresh coat?

Tell us about the job. We'll come out, look at it in person, and give you a written estimate for free.
