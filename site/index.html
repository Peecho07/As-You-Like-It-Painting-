How a job runs

Most of a good paint job happens before the paint comes out.

Prep is where cheap jobs cut corners. It's also where we spend the most time.

  1. Free estimate visit

    We walk the property with you, talk colors and sheen, and note any repairs.

  2. Written estimate

    You get a clear written scope and price. What's included is spelled out.

  3. Protect and prep

    Masking, drop cloths, caulking, sanding, patching, and priming where it's needed.

  4. Finish and walkthrough

    Finish coats, cleanup, and a final walkthrough together before we call it done.
