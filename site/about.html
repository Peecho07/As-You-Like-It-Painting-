What 40 years teaches you

Paint is the easy part. What makes a job last is everything that happens first: the scraping, caulking, sanding, patching, and priming that nobody sees when it's done right.

That's the part we refuse to rush. It's why homeowners, condo boards, and property managers across Palm Beach and Martin County keep calling us back, and why so much of our work comes from referrals.
