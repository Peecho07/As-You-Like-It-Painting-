Financing

Paint now, pay over time.

We've partnered with Acorn Finance so you don't have to wait on a project that can't wait, like peeling exterior paint before storm season.

  1. Get your estimate

    We price the job first so you know the amount to finance.

  2. Check offers with Acorn

    Fill out one short application on Acorn Finance's site to see offers from their lenders.

  3. Pick a plan, book the job

    Choose the offer that fits and we'll get you on the schedule.

Check financing options Get an estimate first

Financing is provided by Acorn Finance and its lending partners. As You Like It Painting is not a lender. All loans subject to credit approval.

Does checking my options affect my credit?

Acorn Finance lets you check offers from its lending partners. Their site explains how pre-qualification works and whether it affects your credit.

What jobs can I finance?

Any project we quote, from a single room to a full exterior repaint or cabinet refinishing.

Who do I talk to about loan terms?

Loan terms come from Acorn Finance's lending partners, not from us. Questions about rates or payments go to them. Questions about the job come to us.

Ready for a fresh coat?

Tell us about the job. We'll come out, look at it in person, and give you a written estimate for free.
